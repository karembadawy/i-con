i-con: tidy up your menu bar
Needs macOS 27.


INSTALL

1. Drag i-con onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open i-con from your Applications folder (not from this window).


FIRST LAUNCH

i-con isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer, so macOS blocks it the first time.

1. Open i-con. When macOS says it can't verify the app, close that message.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click "Open Anyway" next to i-con.
3. Enter your password, then click Open.

You only have to do this once.


PERMISSIONS

- Accessibility (required): lets i-con find, hide, and open menu bar icons. When macOS asks, open System Settings and turn on i-con. (On some versions this list is called "Device Control & Data Access".) If i-con keeps asking after you turned it on, click Restart in its popup.
- Screen Recording (optional): shows the real menu bar icons in the popup instead of app icons. Click Allow at the bottom of the popup.


HOW TO USE

- Click the arrow (a small down chevron) in the menu bar to open the popup.
- Click Edit, then drag icons between "Hidden" and "Menu bar".
- Click a hidden icon to open it. It hides again when its menu closes.
- Gear menu: Launch at Login, Refresh Icons, Quit.


GOOD TO KNOW

- Don't Command-drag icons out of the menu bar: macOS deletes them. To get one back, use System Settings > Menu Bar or that app's settings.
- While any icon is hidden, macOS also hides Now Playing and Fast User Switching. Both are available in Control Center.
- Clicking the clock still opens Notification Center. Your hidden icons show until you close it.
- An app's icons hide and show together.
- Keep your apps in the Applications folder. While i-con hides anything, macOS also hides icons of apps installed elsewhere.
- Quitting i-con brings every icon back.
