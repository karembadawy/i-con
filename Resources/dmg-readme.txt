i-con: tidy up your menu bar
Needs macOS 27 on a MacBook with a notch.


INSTALL

1. Drag i-con onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open i-con from your Applications folder (not from this window).


FIRST LAUNCH

i-con isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer, so macOS blocks it the first time.

1. Open i-con. When macOS says it can't verify the app, close that message.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click "Open Anyway" next to i-con.
3. Enter your password, then click Open.

You only have to do this once. i-con needs no other permissions.


HOW TO USE

- i-con adds a small mark to the menu bar. Icons to the left of the mark are tucked behind macOS's « button.
- Click « to see them. Click » to put them away again.
- To choose what's tucked away, hold Command and drag an icon across the mark.
- Click the mark for Launch at Login and Quit.


GOOD TO KNOW

- Keep icons inside the menu bar while you drag them: macOS deletes icons that are dropped below it. To get one back, use System Settings > Menu Bar or that app's settings.
- On a screen without a notch there's usually room for everything, so nothing is tucked away there.
- When an app with many menus is in front, the menu bar can get so full that macOS tucks the mark away too. It's still behind «.
- Quitting i-con brings every icon back.
